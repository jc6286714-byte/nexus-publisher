Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Nexus Publisher ("the App") handles and why.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open_id, display name and avatar URL. Used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: used only to upload the videos you choose to your TikTok drafts (scope video.upload).

Where it is stored

All data is stored locally on the computer running the App, in a file readable only by your operating-system user. The App has no server and no database of its own; nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

Videos

Videos you select are read from your computer and uploaded directly to TikTok. The App does not copy them elsewhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Clicking Disconnect revokes the TikTok token and deletes your profile and tokens from the App. Deleting the App's data file removes everything else, including the upload history. You can also remove the App's access from TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country).
