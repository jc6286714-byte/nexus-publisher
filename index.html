Nexus Publisher

Nexus Publisher is a desktop tool that lets a creator send their own locally rendered short videos (math, technology and AI explainers made with Manim and Remotion) to their TikTok account's drafts.

How it works

  1. The creator runs Nexus Publisher on their own computer.
  2. They click Connect TikTok and sign in with TikTok (Login Kit). The app shows the connected account's name and avatar.
  3. They pick a rendered video and click Send to TikTok drafts. The video is uploaded to their TikTok inbox via the Content Posting API.
  4. They open the TikTok app, add the caption and sound, and publish it themselves. Nexus Publisher never posts publicly on its own.
